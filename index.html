ABOUT ME

Meet the Senior Flutter Developer behind the screen, building apps with passion and precision.

As a Senior Flutter Developer, I specialize in building high-performance, feature-rich mobile applications for iOS and Android across taxi and ride-hailing, travel and booking, e-commerce and delivery, telemedicine, and CCTV monitoring. My production work also incorporates practical AI capabilities, including AI-driven driver matching and ETA prediction in ride-hailing platforms, face detection, passenger head-counting, and predictive bus ETAs in travel applications, AI-assisted symptom triage in telemedicine solutions, and intelligent person and vehicle detection in CCTV monitoring systems, along with AI-powered smart replies where relevant.

I am proficient in Flutter & Dart, implementing clean architecture and state management solutions such as BLoC, Provider, Riverpod and GetX. I have extensive experience integrating RESTful APIs, Firebase services, Google Maps API, Google Map tracking and Geofencing, Flame, Google Mobile Ads, third-party SDKs, and modern AI stacks — OpenAI and Gemini APIs with function calling and streaming chat UIs, plus ML Kit and TensorFlow Lite for on-device intelligence — always focused on delivering scalable, maintainable, and production-ready solutions.

I'm driven by a desire to deliver innovative solutions and collaborate closely with clients to transform their visions into digital reality. I also mentor junior developers, review code, and work directly with clients from kickoff to store release.
